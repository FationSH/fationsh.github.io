Privacy Policy

App: «Δίπλωμα Τεστ ΚΟΚ» (Greek driving-licence theory test, Android, com.fsharp.diploma)
Last updated: 9 October 2026

Contact

The app «Δίπλωμα Τεστ ΚΟΚ» is developed by Fation Shehaj. If you have any question about privacy, contact me at fsharp.source@gmail.com.

Accounts and personal data

The app has no accounts and does not ask you to sign up. The app itself does not collect any personal data.

What is stored on your device

The app stores only the following on your phone:

  • your settings (theme, licence category, daily goal)
  • onboarding flags
  • your study progress (answers, exam history, daily activity)

This data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. All of it is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings. «Μηδενισμός προόδου» (Reset progress) in «Πληροφορίες» removes your answers and exam history. Your daily activity (streak) and settings are kept.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. When required, Google's consent form (UMP) is shown on first use.

  • With your consent: AdMob may process the device's advertising ID and device data for personalised ads and for measurement.
  • Without consent: only non-personalised ads are shown, or no ads at all. Where consent is not required by law, Google may show personalised ads according to its own policy.

You can change your choices in «Πληροφορίες → Ρυθμίσεις διαφημίσεων» (About → Ad settings), where this option is offered (it appears when the consent rules require it). Google acts as an independent controller of the data it processes for ads; see How Google uses data. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children. Its audience is people aged 16 and over.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page and the "Last updated" date at the top will be updated. For questions, contact fsharp.source@gmail.com.
